# Transfer Strategy — best school, most money (Fall 2027 entry)

_Written 2026-10-27. Rules and deadlines are what each school or foundation published as of this
date. Verify each one at its source before acting; they change every cycle._

## The bottom line

1. **Money is not what's stopping you from going to an elite school. Admission is.** With a FAFSA SAI of
   -1500, the schools that meet 100% of demonstrated need (most of the Ivies, plus Amherst,
   Williams, Stanford, MIT and others) would charge you close to $0 for tuition, room and board. Your
   Michigan residency doesn't matter there. Residency only matters for **public Michigan schools**:
   UM Ann Arbor, UM-Flint and MSU.
2. **Jack Kent Cooke, the #1 item in `APPLICATION_QUEUE.md`, almost certainly excludes you.** Its FAQ
   says students who have previously attended a four-year institution are not eligible. The only
   exception is someone who did not finish a semester's worth of credit. You finished 15 credits at
   UM-Flint in Fall 2025. Send one email to `scholarships@jkcf.org` before the **Dec 9, 2026** deadline
   to confirm, then drop it from the queue. The scout now screens this rule out automatically (see
   "Repo changes").
3. **Your gap-semester instinct is right, and the reason is in UM's own rules.** UM's residency
   guidelines list this as a circumstance that does *not* establish residence: _attended an
   out-of-state high school **and** has been involved in educational pursuits for the majority of
   the time since high school graduation._ That describes you today: Georgia high school, then
   continuously enrolled. The current plan's part-time Mott "residency semester" in Winter 2027 is
   still an educational pursuit, so it works *against* residency, not for it.
4. **Fall 2026 grades are the last ones most schools will see before deciding.** Treat every class
   this semester as if the competition is the Cornell transfer pool, not your Mott classmates.
5. **Wrestling is your biggest unused lever.** Ivy schools don't give athletic scholarships, but a
   coach who supports your application is the strongest admissions boost there is. Six Ivies have
   wrestling: Brown, Columbia, Cornell, Harvard, Penn and Princeton.

## 1. The money math

| Path | Tuition paid by | What you need |
|---|---|---|
| Ivy / elite private that meets full need | The school's need-based grant plus Pell | Admission, plus the CSS Profile and FAFSA. Residency is irrelevant. |
| UM Ann Arbor (in-state) | Go Blue Guarantee (free tuition) plus Pell toward housing | Michigan residency, family income and assets ≤ $125K, full-time enrollment |
| UM-Flint (in-state) | Go Blue Guarantee plus Pell | Michigan residency, **3.5 cumulative GPA across all colleges**, full-time, income ≤ $125K |
| UM-Flint (out-of-state) | Pell plus the $2,500/yr transfer scholarship | ~$10K/yr gap. This is the red number on the dashboard. |

Things to watch:
- **Go Blue counts transfer credit against you.** Every 15 transferred credits uses up one
  semester of eligibility. Your 32 AP credits may count. Ask UM-Flint financial aid how many
  terms you'd have left.
- **Your new job shows up on future aid forms.** FAFSA uses prior-prior-year income: 2027–28 aid
  looks at 2025 income, and 2028–29 aid looks at **2026** income, which includes this job.
  Dependent students can earn up to about $11.5K before it starts raising the SAI, and roughly
  half of every dollar above that counts. The CSS Profile, which privates use, also looks at
  parent income and assets. A higher income is worth it, but plan for a smaller Pell later.
- **The Michigan Achievement Scholarship is out.** It requires graduating from a Michigan high
  school, and you graduated in Georgia.

## 2. In-state residency: the gap semester decision

**What UM's Regents' guidelines weigh:** where you live, work and attend school; where you
*have* lived, worked and attended school; and **where your parents live**. Living in Michigan for
a year does not by itself establish residency. Factors listed against you include an out-of-state
high school with mostly educational pursuits since graduating, and having attended a UM campus as
a nonresident. Decisions use only the facts in place by the filing deadline for the term.

**Time since high school graduation (May 2025) to the start of Fall 2027, about 27 months:**

| Winter 2027 plan | Months enrolled | Months not enrolled | "Majority in educational pursuits"? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Part-time Mott semester (current plan) | ~16 | ~11 | **Yes.** Works against you. |
| Full-time work, not enrolled | ~12 | ~15 | **No.** The balance tips your way. |

**Recommendation:** make Winter 2027 a **work semester**, full-time W-2 employment in Michigan,
**not** a part-time Mott semester. Before you commit, call or email the **UM Residency Office**
(ro.umich.edu/tuition-residency/residency) and ask them to look at your specific facts. They decide
UM-Flint residency too.

What to stack up between now and the filing deadline:
- Full-time employment in Michigan (an offer letter or pay stubs showing hours)
- A Michigan driver's license, voter registration and vehicle registration
- A lease or housing in your name in Michigan
- A 2026 Michigan resident income tax return, filed early
- Bank accounts and a phone plan with a Michigan address

What the gap semester costs:
- One Pell disbursement at Mott (~$3,500). Pell lifetime eligibility isn't lost, only deferred.
- The 8 Winter-2027 credits in `pathway.js`. Most are padding, so the degree barely moves.
- No spring grades for Ivy transfer readers. Fall 2026 becomes your final transcript, so it has
  to be strong.

**The single biggest unknown: where do your parents live, and do they claim you as a
dependent?** If they live in Michigan, residency is probably easy and most of this section doesn't
apply. If they're in Georgia, it's the main obstacle, because you're a dependent for aid purposes
until you're 24 regardless of who pays your bills.

## 3. Grades: the honest picture

- UM-Flint Fall 2025: **3.92** (15 cr). Mott Winter 2026: **3.70** (15 cr). Cumulative ≈ **3.81**.
- Straight A's in Fall 2026 (14 cr) bring it to about **3.87**. That's competitive for UM Ann Arbor
  and a reach-but-real number for Cornell, Penn and Columbia. Admitted Ivy transfers usually sit
  at 3.9 or higher.
- **MATH 170 (Calculus I) matters most.** Every STEM transfer reader looks at calculus first.
- You said you rise to the level of the competition. So pick a competitor: before each exam, look
  at the Cornell CALS transfer profile, not the class average.
- Go to office hours twice this semester for the professors you'd ask for recommendations. Ivy
  transfer applications need **two college instructor recommendations**. Pick your two now.

## 4. School list (apply to all tiers for Fall 2027)

Admission rates below are third-party estimates. Treat them as rough.

**Reach (residency doesn't matter, full need met, ~$0 at SAI -1500):**
- **Cornell** (~14% transfer, highest of the Ivies; CALS Biological Sciences). Deadline around
  **March 15**. Has wrestling.
- **Columbia College / SEAS** (~10–15%). Use the regular transfer route, *not* the School of General
  Studies, whose aid is less generous. Has wrestling.
- **Penn** (~5–6%). Has wrestling.
- **Princeton** (~3%). Its transfer program explicitly favors community-college and low-income
  students, which fits your profile. Has wrestling.
- **Brown, Harvard**. Both have wrestling.
- **Amherst**. Small, but the best-known private for community-college transfers, with full need met.
- Confirm on each school's site that transfers get the **same need-based aid** as first-years before
  you pay any application fee. Most Ivies give fee waivers at your SAI.

**Target:**
- **UM Ann Arbor** (~35% transfer overall; Michigan residents admitted at higher rates). If you have
  residency, Go Blue makes tuition free. This is the best realistic outcome, with Big Ten wrestling
  and the PIBS/DCMB labs on the same campus as your PhD goal.
- **Michigan State**. Has D1 wrestling and transfer merit aid.

**Floor (already secured):**
- **UM-Flint**. Go Blue if you're a resident and have a 3.5 GPA; otherwise Pell plus $2,500 plus
  the dashboard gap. The current plan in `pathway.js` already covers this path.
