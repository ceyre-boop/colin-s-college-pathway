# Jack Kent Cooke Foundation Undergraduate Transfer Scholarship

My FAFSA SAI is negative fifteen hundred. Maximum need, the government's arithmetic, not mine.

Mine went differently. Given that number, the question wasn't whether I'd finish a degree — it was which parts of a degree I was actually paying for. So I looked at it the way I'd look at any system with a cost constraint: find the load-bearing components, find the ones that are just convention, and stop paying for convention. Organic chemistry is load-bearing. Where I take it is not. I'm finishing prerequisites at Mott Community College and transferring back to UM-Flint in Fall 2027 for the biology degree, in the labs that actually matter for the work I want to do.

People hear community college and hear a smaller ambition. What it actually was, was the first real engineering decision I ever made about my own life.

And I didn't slow down while I made it. I'm the founding AI engineer at TABOOST — the largest social media agency in the United States, five million in profit last year — where the system I built reads and routes over two hundred emails a day across sixteen inboxes. I also built Alta Investments alone at eighteen, a quantitative trading system that trades live on a Sharpe of 1.25 with probability of ruin held at zero. I didn't wait for a bachelor's degree to start doing the work. The degree is the next component, installed on schedule.

Here's why the biology half isn't negotiable, though, and why I'm transferring rather than just continuing to code.

When I was sixteen my uncle let me sit down at the treatment planning workstation in his radiation oncology clinic in North Dakota. On screen was a brain in grayscale, sliced thin. My job was to drag a cursor around the tumor's edge on each slice so the radiation plan would know where to aim. It took me eleven minutes to do one. There were forty more. Somewhere behind that gray smudge was a person with an appointment.

A patient's treatment margin shouldn't depend on how steady a sixteen-year-old's hand is, and an oncologist's attention is too expensive to spend tracing outlines. The segmentation models that exist aren't good enough for the clinics that need them most, and the space between the papers and the treatment room is exactly where I want to work.

You can't build that with only the CS half. You have to know what you're looking at.

That's the whole transfer, in one sentence: I already know how to build the thing. I'm going back to learn what it's about.
